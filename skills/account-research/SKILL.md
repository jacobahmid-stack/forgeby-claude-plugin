name: account-research
description: Use when I ask what we know about one account, to brief me on a company, or before I walk into a first conversation.
---

Ask which account if I have not named one. Then read Forgeby first: `query_corpus` or `account_context`, then `account_contacts`, `pipeline`, and `whats_changed`.

Return everything Forgeby holds. Each fact needs a source and a date. Name the gaps. Do not invent a fact.

If two companies match, ask one question, then stop.

Do not send. Do not enrol. Do not write a record.

Do not name a CRM vendor the workspace has not connected.
