name: morning-brief
description: Use when I ask who to call today, what to say this morning, or for a morning brief before I open the book.
---

Read Forgeby first. Call `who_to_call`. If the desk has due mail, call `due_draft`. If I name a date window, call `accounts_changed`.

For each account, say who to call, why now, and what to say. Every fact needs a source and a date. If a field is missing, say so. Do not invent a reason.

If more than one desk could be meant, ask one question, then stop.

Do not send. Do not enrol. Do not write a record. If a follow-up should go out, prepare it and stop at the approval link.

Do not name a CRM vendor the workspace has not connected.
