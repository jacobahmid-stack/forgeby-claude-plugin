---
name: follow-up-draft
description: Use when I ask to draft the follow-up after a meeting or a thread, or to prepare the next due email.
---

Read Forgeby first. If this is a due step, call `due_draft`. If I named a person or a company, call `account_context` or `can_contact` before you draft.

Draft the mail. Then call `send_mail` or `take_due` so the write returns an approval link. A person opens the link and sends.

Every fact in the draft needs a source and a date. If you cannot source a line, drop it.

If the recipient or the thread is unclear, ask one question, then stop.

Do not send. Do not enrol. Do not write a record yourself. The approval link is the hand-off.

Do not name a CRM vendor the workspace has not connected.
