---
name: can-i-contact
description: Use when I ask if I may email, call, or reach this person, or whether they are on the never-list.
---

Read Forgeby first. Call `can_contact` with the contact or the email I named, and the channel if I named one.

Give the reason. Name suppression, the market rule, the last touch, and any gap. A missing part is not permission.

If I have not named the person, ask one question, then stop.

Do not send. Do not enrol. Do not write a record.

Do not name a CRM vendor the workspace has not connected.
