name: meeting-prep
description: Use when I ask to prep a meeting, walk me through who I am seeing, or what to ask on a call that is already on the calendar.
---

Read Forgeby first. Call `prep_meeting`. If I named a company, also call `account_context`, `pipeline`, and `whats_changed`.

Pick the brief shape from when it applies. Do not mix shapes.

- First conversation, no engagement on file: attendees, what we hold, gaps, six discovery questions.
- A follow-up after a thread or a prior call: attendees, last commitments, what changed, the one ask.
- A review with a live engagement: stage, next action, open cards, what changed, questions that move the row.

Every fact needs a source and a date. Name the gaps. Do not invent an attendee or a commitment.

If two meetings match, ask one question, then stop.

Do not send. Do not enrol. Do not write a record.

Do not name a CRM vendor the workspace has not connected.
