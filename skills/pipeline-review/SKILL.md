---
name: pipeline-review
description: Use when I ask which deals are stalled, what is at risk, or what the next step is on the pipeline.
---

Read Forgeby first. Call `pipeline`. Rank stalled and at-risk rows from stage, next action, and the note already on the desk. Say the next step that is already written, or say it is missing.

Every fact needs a source and a date. Do not invent a next step.

If I have not said whose book, ask one question, then stop.

Do not send. Do not enrol. Do not write a record. A stage change is `engagement_update` and returns an approval link.

Do not name a CRM vendor the workspace has not connected.
